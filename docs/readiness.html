    Why Q-FINEX

    A benchmark only a network can build

    A readiness index is only as good as the institutions, research and testing behind it. Q-FINEX brings all three together.

    The network

    Convened by the Global Finance & Technology Network (GFTN), whose global network of forums, including the Singapore FinTech Festival, connects financial and technology institutions, regulators, policymakers and partners. That breadth of relationships and market insight is what a credible benchmark needs.

    The research

    Developed with The University of Western Australia’s Quantum Information, Simulation and Algorithms (QUISA) research group, drawing on its global network of research collaborators, quantum industry partners and projects, for independent, evidence-led assessment.

    The testbed

    Connected to the Q-FINEX testbed, so a readiness gap can become a defined problem, a test and a result.

    Founding partners

    Build the benchmark with us

    The Index brings financial institutions, quantum and security companies, researchers and regulators to one table. Founding partners help shape it, and stand with it at the launch in Perth.
